## Before you film — read this once

- **Orientation:** Vertical (9:16), phone held upright. Lock focus/exposure by tapping the part on screen before you start talking.
- **Lighting:** Film near a window or shop bay door in daylight if possible. Avoid single overhead fluorescent only — it casts green/yellow tint. If shooting indoors, turn on extra shop lights and face the part toward the light, not backlit.
- **Steady:** Prop the phone against something (toolbox, shelf, stack of boxes) or use a cheap tripod/clamp if you have one. Handheld is fine for the "pick up and show" shots — just brace your elbows against your body.
- **Background:** Plain-ish shop background (workbench, shelving) is fine and on-brand — don't over-clean it, "real shop" is the vibe.
- **Audio:** Talk close to the phone mic (arm's length or less). If the shop is noisy, do it as text-overlay + voiceover recorded in a quieter corner instead of live sync sound.
- **Do NOT say "tested" or "bench-tested"** for any of these — there's no confirmed test data. Use language like "pulled from a running vehicle," "clean condition," "good connector," etc. — visual/condition claims only, not functional guarantees.
- **Do NOT state a price on camera or on screen.** Every script ends on "message for price" or equivalent. Price is Ralph's call, not scripted here.
- **One-off urgency is real** — say so plainly, don't oversell it. Each of these is a single salvage unit; once it's sold there isn't another one.

Each script below is self-contained: film it, no editing skill needed beyond trimming clips together in order and adding the text overlays listed.

## Filming order suggestion (for one weekend session)

Batch all 5 in one sitting to save setup time:
1. Set up the phone rig once (propped/clamped, consistent background, consistent lighting) — don't move it between parts.
2. Film all 5 "part on bench" and "rotate in hand" shots back to back, swapping parts only.
3. Film all 5 talking-to-camera segments back to back (easier to stay in the same energy/tone).
4. Film all 5 "hold up one finger / 1 in stock" close-outs back to back.
5. Sort footage into 5 folders by vehicle before handing off for editing.
