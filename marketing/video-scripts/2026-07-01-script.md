## Filming tips
- Shoot near the shop's bay door or a window for natural light, avoid harsh overhead fluorescents alone.
- Prop your phone against something steady rather than hand-holding — a toolbox or shelf works.
- Vertical orientation (9:16) for TikTok/Reels.
- Do shots 2-3 times each if you can — gives you options to pick the cleanest take when editing.
