# Video Script — Pre-Holiday Road Trip Checklist (Tires, Coolant, Belts)

**Date:** Friday, July 3, 2026
**Platform(s):** TikTok (primary), Instagram Reels (repost)
**Runtime target:** 20-30 seconds
**Source:** `marketing/strategy/weekly-calendar-2026-07-01.md`, Fri Jul 3 row

---

## Hook Line

"Before you drive 6 hours this weekend, check these 3 things — takes less time than filling up your tank."

## Shot List

| Shot # | What's Shown | Angle/Distance | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Presenter (Ralph or staff) standing at open hood/bay door, says hook line to camera | Medium shot, vertical, eye level | 3s |
| 2 | Hand pressing tire tread with a coin or checking tread wear bar, then tire pressure gauge on valve stem | Close-up, phone angled down at tire | 5s |
| 3 | Hand opening coolant reservoir cap (engine OFF and cool), pointing to fill line | Close-up, top-down on engine bay | 5s |
| 4 | Hand flexing/pinching a belt or hose to show it's firm (no cracks) vs. a cracked one for comparison if available | Close-up, angled into engine bay | 5s |
| 5 | Presenter back to camera, quick recap: "Tires. Coolant. Belts. Ten minutes, way better weekend." | Medium shot, vertical | 4s |
| 6 | Shop signage or storefront, on-screen text with shop name/hours | Wide shot, vertical | 3s |

## On-Screen Text

- Opening: "3 things to check before your road trip"
- Shot 2: "TIRES — tread + pressure"
- Shot 3: "COOLANT — check the level (engine cool!)"
- Shot 4: "BELTS — feel for cracks"
- Closing: "10 minutes. Way better weekend. [Shop name] — stop by before you go."

## CTA

"Not sure what to look for? Swing by before the weekend — we'll do a quick check for you."

## Audio Vibe

Upbeat, quick-cut energy — matches the fast checklist pacing. Two current trending options found via search this week (verify still live in TikTok's Trending Sounds tab, filtered to "approved for business," before filming):
1. **"Bangladesh" by Ian McConnell** — deadpan, escalating-demand track currently popular with business accounts; could work as a playful hook if repurposed loosely, but isn't a natural narrative fit for a checklist video — lower priority for this piece.
2. **"Guess the sound" format** — someone mimics a sound (e.g., a tool, an engine, a hose hiss) before cutting to the real thing. This is a strong fit for an auto shop and could be a good alternate hook for shot 1 (e.g., mimic a squealing belt sound, then cut to "that's what a worn belt sounds like — check yours before you go").

If neither trend is a clean fit by filming day, fall back to a generic upbeat instrumental — don't force a trend that doesn't match the content.

## Filming Tips

- **Lighting:** Shoot near the shop's front window or with the bay door open for natural daylight — avoid shooting under a single overhead fluorescent, it flattens the video.
- **Stability:** Prop the phone against a toolbox, shelf, or use a cheap clamp mount — no tripod needed, just keep it still during each shot.
- **Orientation:** Vertical (9:16) — this is for TikTok/Reels, not YouTube.
- **Engine safety:** Only open the coolant reservoir cap when the engine is cool — do not remove a radiator cap on a hot engine on camera; make sure this is visibly a cool/off engine for both accuracy and safety.
- **Keep it moving:** Each shot is short (3-5s) — cut fast, don't linger, this format lives or dies on pace.
